A sharp cutoff with a silent plateau up to Nyquist is flagged as a likely Transcode (with estimated source bitrate). A rolloff that instead rides right up to Nyquist is marked Resampled — a lossless source that was sample-rate-converted (e.g. hi-res downsampled to 44.1 kHz), not a lossy encode.
